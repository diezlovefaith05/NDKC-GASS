Priority Classifications & Response Times

Requests are processed based on urgency and safety impact.

Emergency

Repairs that pose an immediate threat to life, safety, or property.

  • Electrical short circuits
  • Broken water pipes / flooding
  • Structural damage & hazards
  • Fire safety equipment malfunction
Response Time Immediate (Within 24 Hours)
Urgent

Repairs that significantly affect office operations without immediate danger.

  • Air-conditioning unit failure
  • Plumbing leaks
  • Broken windows
  • Damaged essential office furniture
Response Time 2–5 Working Days
Routine

Minor repairs and scheduled preventive maintenance works.

  • Light bulb replacements
  • Door & cabinet adjustments
  • Painting touch-ups
  • Ceiling tile replacement
Response Time 10–15 Working Days

Standard Repair Request Workflow

10-step process from issue identification to completion confirmation.

01

Identify Problem

Spot facility defect or maintenance need.

02

Submit Form

Complete online request form (with photo).

03

GASS Logs Request

Receive, record, and tag submission.

04

Site Inspection

Assess situation & determine requirements.

05

Prioritize & Approve

Classify priority and approve work order.

06

Schedule Works

Assign personnel & schedule window.

07

Repair Execution

Maintenance carries out required work.

08

Final Inspection

Verify quality and completeness.

09

Office Acceptance

Requesting office confirms satisfaction.

10

Request Closed

Log updated and archived in system.

Key Guidelines:

• Requests must be endorsed by the Head of Office (except emergencies)[cite: 2].

• Include complete details and attach photos whenever possible[cite: 2].

• Do not undertake repairs or modifications without prior approval from GASS[cite: 2].
