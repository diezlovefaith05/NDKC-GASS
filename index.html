FACILITY REPAIR REQUEST GUIDELINES

PURPOSE

To provide a standardized procedure for requesting and processing repairs to ensure campus facilities remain safe, functional, and well-maintained.

WHO MAY SUBMIT REQUESTS?

  • Academic Departments
  • Administrative Offices
  • Support Units
  • Other Authorized Campus Offices

GENERAL GUIDELINES

1

Submit a Facility Repair Request Form to GASS. Use our online web portal requisition form.

2

Requests must be endorsed by the Head of Office (except in emergency situations).

3

Provide complete details of the problem and its precise campus location.
