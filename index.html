FACILITY REPAIR REQUEST GUIDELINES & PORTAL

PURPOSE

To provide a standardized procedure for requesting and processing repairs to ensure campus facilities remain safe, functional, and well-maintained.

WHO MAY SUBMIT REQUESTS?

  • Academic Departments
  • Administrative Offices
  • Support Units
  • Other Authorized Campus Offices

GENERAL GUIDELINES

1

Submit a Facility Repair Request Form to GASS.

2

Requests must be endorsed by the Head of Office (except in emergencies).

3

Provide complete details of the problem and its exact campus location.

4

Include photos whenever possible to expedite initial inspection.

5

Do not undertake repairs or modifications without prior approval from GASS.

SLIDES 08 & 09

REPAIR WORKFLOW & PRIORITY CLASSIFICATIONS

10-Step Standard Repair Request Workflow

End-to-End Lifecycle
STEP 01

Identify Facility Problem

Locate defect or damage in classroom or office.

STEP 02

Submit Request Form

Fill out repair form with photos and location.

STEP 03

GASS Receives & Logs

Ticket logged into central tracking database.

STEP 04

Site Inspection

Technical team conducts initial assessment.

STEP 05

Prioritize & Approve

GASS approves work order & urgency level.

STEP 06

Schedule Repair Works

Personnel & materials assigned to schedule.

STEP 07

Repair Execution

Maintenance personnel conduct repair works.

STEP 08

Final Inspection

GASS inspector verifies quality of work.

STEP 09

Office Acceptance

Requesting office confirms completion.

STEP 10

Request Closed

Ticket archived with full job sign-off.

Priority Classification Matrix

PRIORITY A

Emergency Repairs

Repairs that pose an immediate threat to life, safety, or property.

Examples:
  • Electrical short circuits
  • Broken water pipes causing flooding
  • Structural damage
  • Fire safety equipment malfunction
  • Broken doors compromising security
Target Response Time Immediate (Within 24 Hours)
PRIORITY B

Urgent Repairs

Repairs that significantly affect office operations but do not present immediate danger.

Examples:
  • Air-conditioning failure
  • Plumbing leaks
  • Broken windows
  • Damaged office furniture essential for work
Target Response Time 2–5 Working Days
PRIORITY C

Routine Repairs

Minor repairs and scheduled preventive maintenance works.

Examples:
  • Replacement of light bulbs
  • Door adjustments
  • Cabinet repairs
  • Painting touch-ups
  • Ceiling tile replacement
Target Response Time 10–15 Working Days
SLIDE 10

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Requesting Office

  • Report facility issues promptly.
  • Submit a complete Repair Request Form.
  • Provide access to the work area.
  • Confirm satisfactory completion of repairs.

GASS Office

  • Receive and record repair requests.
  • Conduct inspections and determine repair requirements.
  • Prioritize, schedule, and monitor repair activities.
  • Coordinate materials, manpower, and external contractors when necessary.

Maintenance Personnel

  • Perform repairs safely and efficiently.
  • Follow institutional standards and safety protocols.
  • Report additional defects identified during repair works.
KEY REMINDER

Emergency repairs should be reported immediately to GASS (or Campus Security after office hours), followed by the submission of the Repair Request Form on the next working day.
