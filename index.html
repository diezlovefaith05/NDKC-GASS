Section 05

FACILITY REPAIR REQUEST GUIDELINES

Purpose

To provide a standardized procedure for requesting and processing repairs to ensure campus facilities remain safe, functional, and well-maintained[cite: 4].

Who May Submit Requests?

  • Academic Departments
  • Administrative Offices
  • Support Units
  • Other Authorized Campus Offices

General Guidelines

  1. Submit a Facility Repair Request Form to GASS[cite: 4].
  2. Requests must be endorsed by the Head of Office (except in emergencies)[cite: 4].
  3. Provide complete details of the problem and its location[cite: 4].
  4. Include photos whenever possible[cite: 4].
  5. Do not undertake repairs or modifications without prior approval from GASS[cite: 4].

Priority A

Emergency Repairs

Repairs that pose an immediate threat to life, safety, or property[cite: 4].

  • Electrical short circuits
  • Broken water pipes / flooding
  • Structural damage
  • Fire safety equipment malfunction
  • Broken doors compromising security
Response Time Immediate (Within 24 Hours)
Priority B

Urgent Repairs

Repairs that significantly affect office operations but do not present immediate danger[cite: 4].

  • Air-conditioning failure
  • Plumbing leaks
  • Broken windows
  • Damaged essential office furniture
Response Time 2–5 Working Days
Priority C

Routine Repairs

Minor repairs and preventive maintenance[cite: 4].

  • Replacement of light bulbs
  • Door adjustments
  • Cabinet repairs
  • Painting touch-ups
  • Ceiling tile replacement
Response Time 10–15 Working Days
Section 07

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Requesting Office

  • Report facility issues promptly[cite: 4].
  • Submit a complete Repair Request Form[cite: 4].
  • Provide access to the work area[cite: 4].
  • Confirm satisfactory completion of repairs[cite: 4].

GASS Office

  • Receive and record repair requests[cite: 4].
  • Conduct inspections & determine requirements[cite: 4].
  • Prioritize, schedule, and monitor repair activities[cite: 4].
  • Coordinate materials, manpower, and contractors[cite: 4].

Maintenance Personnel

  • Perform repairs safely and efficiently[cite: 4].
  • Follow institutional standards & safety protocols[cite: 4].
  • Report additional defects identified during works[cite: 4].

KEY REMINDER:

Emergency repairs should be reported immediately to GASS (or Campus Security after office hours), followed by the submission of the Repair Request Form on the next working day[cite: 4].
